## Quarry clue choices for owner/Noor — all **PROPOSED**

Each option uses a landmark already authored. It becomes a reason only if the owner/Noor chooses the observation and approves Lorenzo's inference; mere visibility does not prove a connection to Joseph or the roots.

1. **PROPOSED — chalk arrow as a separate Ravens lead.** Lorenzo observes the existing “Ravens” chalk and south arrow on barn [1] (`village.md`, environmental story). The hoofprints point northeast; the arrow explicitly points the wrong way for the herd. Lorenzo could investigate the Ravens lead as a second question after recording the herd trace. This does **not** establish who drew the arrow, whether it is true, or that Joseph went south. A chosen version needs a player-facing inspection before the quarry objective.
2. **PROPOSED — square sightline to the quarry.** From the square, Lorenzo sees the already-authored four white quarry cuts and black diagonal conveyor (`ravens-tunnels.md`, distant landmarks). He could mark that physical destination while investigating the Ravens' old passages. This is a strong orientation cue, but alone it gives no causal proof; an approved reason to suspect that site still needs to be stated or established from existing evidence.
3. **PROPOSED — bridge confirmation.** Lorenzo can see the same quarry terraces from the existing stone bridge, with the conveyor descending from the portal. This could confirm he is approaching the visible site selected under option 1 or 2. It is a route confirmation, **not** a standalone village clue; it should not silently stand in for the missing motivation.

**Decision requested:** choose whether the existing chalk arrow is a legitimate Ravens lead, or identify another already-authored observation that connects the missing-person inquiry to the visible quarry. Then choose whether Moreno's assignment is only communicated by the Day 2 card or staged so Lorenzo can plausibly hear it. Until decided, the options above remain drafts and must not become a required objective or recorded line. Southbound direction is already approved, independently of these choices.
