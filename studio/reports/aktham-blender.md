# Aktham: Blender backdrop and village textures (2026-10-18)
1. **valley-dusk**: `scripts/blender/valley_dusk.py` (Cycles CPU, 32 spp, OIDN, Bloom, Kuwahara, grade). Key art `public/art/valley-dusk-key.jpg` is 1920x1080, 87,996 B, render 280 s. Sky `public/art/valley-sky.jpg` is a 4096x2048 equirect, 129,106 B, render 228–599 s. Horizon at v=0.5 (camera pitch 0). Mapping: u = ((bearing−270) mod 360)/360, which is three.js `EquirectangularReflectionMapping`. Measured: sun at 240°/4.3°; cave arch 16 px = 1.41° wide at 45.09°, sill 4.48° (game 1.47°/5.6°); peak 16.5°; WSW skyline 1.8°.
2. **Textures** (`scripts/blender/village_textures.py`, 1024², 300 s bake): dirt-road, grass-dry, wood-planks-barn, roof-tiles-red, stone-wall, hay, fence-wood as `.jpg`. Normals ship for dirt-road, wood-planks-barn, roof-tiles-red and stone-wall (`<name>_normal.jpg`, OpenGL). Roughness ships for dirt-road only (`dirt-road_rough.jpg`).
3. **Seams**: every wrap pair ranks below the 91st percentile against interior pairs in the PNG bake (`art-src/tex/seams.json`). The 2×2 checks are `art-src/tex/<name>-tile2x2.png`. On the JPEGs the wrap pair ranks 0–94% against local block edges; panorama wrap |Δ| is 0.86 levels/channel after a cross-faded 64 px overscan.
4. **Palette**: worst albedo is hay at ΔE 21.0 (limit 25); stone-wall is best at 7.2. Each albedo average equals a villageScene COL colour; the hex values are in `studio/art/prompts/village-textures.md`.
5. **Bytes**: public/art totals 2,495,861 B over 16 files. Projected dist is 3,232,302 B against a 5,242,880 B budget. `node scripts/blender/encode-art.mjs` exits 0. `scripts/check-art.mjs` exits 2 because pngjs is not installed.
6. **Rajaie**: `withTexture` loads only `art/<name>.png`, so it needs a `.jpg` path plus `_normal` and `_rough` slots. TEX_BASE keys and their sRGB bases are listed in `village-textures.md`. Canon says slate roofs, so roof-tiles-red is only for red barn trim.
7. **Known weak spots**: in the key art the herd and Joseph are present but too small and dark to read at 1920, and the roots are only faint.
