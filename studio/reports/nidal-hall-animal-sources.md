# Hall animal gap: source review — Nidal, 2026-10-21

Scope: source research plus incoming goat, deer and dog downloads. No asset was converted, placed in the shipped `public/models` library, or loaded by the game in this pass. The Hall specification in `studio/world/regions/mountain-caves.md` names cows, sheep, goats, horses, deer and a dog. The shipped shelf already has `public/models/animals/cow.glb`, `sheep.glb`, `horse.glb` and `pig.glb`; their Quaternius source and CC0 license are documented in `studio/art/free-assets-manifest.md`. Goats, deer and dog remain absent from that shipped shelf. Do not duplicate the existing four files or mistake the pig for a canon Hall species.

**First source attempt, 2026-10-21:** attempted the 3DAssets goat's published `https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/62/v1/model.glb` URL from this workspace. DNS lookup for `cdn.3dassets.dev` returned no address. Its `https://3dassets.dev/download/harvest-hearth-horned-farm-goat` endpoint returned HTTP 503. No GLB was obtained from that source, so its listing metadata below is unverified locally. The alternative Innerscene goat was subsequently obtained and inspected.

**Alternate goat downloaded, 2026-10-21:** the creator's [Innerscene Horned goat page](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/horned-goat-171680de) states original Innerscene authorship and CC0. Its [direct GLB endpoint](https://www.innerscene.com/api/library/horned-goat-171680de/download) worked without login. The file is staged at `art-incoming/nidal-hall-animals/innerscene-horned-goat.glb`; exact provenance and checksum are in the adjacent `SOURCE-innerscene-horned-goat.md`. GLB v2 signature/length valid; 865,812 bytes, 33,532 triangles, 22 meshes/primitives, 6 materials, 6 textures, no skin or animation. Scene-node-transformed accessor bounds approximately 1.188 × 1.040 × 0.324 m, ground at Y=0. This is a relatively dense static mesh beside the current 562–796 triangle farm animals; use only as an art-review option, not for a 400-animal Hall crowd. No runtime integration or visual validation was done. The failed 3DAssets candidate below remains a research option only.

**Deer and dog downloaded, 2026-10-21:** their Quaternius individual listings expose no-login direct GLB preview files. Both listings mark the models CC0; the creator's pack page independently states CC0/free commercial terms. Staged as `art-incoming/nidal-hall-animals/quaternius-deer.glb` and `quaternius-dog.glb`, with exact URLs, hashes and inspection data in adjacent `SOURCE-quaternius-deer.md` and `SOURCE-quaternius-dog.md`. Deer: 1,007,564 bytes, 2,096 triangles, 7 materials, 0 textures, 46-joint skin, 26 clips including duplicate-prefixed names. Dog: 296,880 bytes, 3,604 triangles, 1 material/texture, 16-joint skin, 8 clips. Both pass GLB v2 length/signature checks and have no required extensions. Static rest-pose accessor bounds are large (deer about 4.27 m tall; dog about 1.90 m tall), so scale, axes, feet and animation require rendered validation before Hall or farmhouse placement. No runtime files were touched.

**Import gate:** save each source page URL and included license with the archive, inspect GLTFLoader parse, bounding box, feet origin, forward/up axes, triangle count, texture/material count and animation clips. Place a small mixed-species review scene first; compare silhouette and color under the Hall light, including whether the single dog reads clearly. Use independent skeleton clones for animated instances. For a full 400-animal Hall, use few visible near-field animated exemplars and static/instanced distant figures with bounded draw calls; asset metadata alone does not prove frame rate. Do not alter the Hall's species or story timing to fit an asset.

**Excluded alternatives:** the [OpenGameArt CC0 goat](https://opengameart.org/content/goat) is OBJ only, unrigged and untextured; [OpenGameArt Deer Female](https://opengameart.org/content/deer-female) is CC0 and rigged but ships Blend/FBX rather than a ready glTF. Both require conversion or extra art work. A [Kenney Animal Pack Redux](https://opengameart.org/content/animal-pack-redux) result lists goat and dog but does not establish a suitable matching ready GLB; avoid importing another entire stylized pack for two species.
