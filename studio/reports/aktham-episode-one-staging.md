## Art tasks that can proceed without story decisions

1. Capture a first-person reference frame at each listed beat and log the camera position, actor feet, obstruction, draw distance and frame rate. Preserve travel lanes and documented compass direction (herd trail north-east, quarry road exits south).
2. For each candidate character GLB, check scale, feet on scene ground, actual idle/walk/speak support and clothing fit before assigning it to a role. The candidate Ottoman GLBs have a different apparent size than current worker GLBs per the asset manifest.
3. Dress only existing environments with local props and materials, then recapture. Use the actual built geometry to determine café/school elevations; zero is only a temporary test value.
4. Identify replacement or additive art for the absent Hall species and any desired visible upload/medical props. Record license and source before integrating. Do not infer that every story prop has a file merely because a procedural representation exists.
