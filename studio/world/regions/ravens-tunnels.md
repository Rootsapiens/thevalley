# Region: The Ravens' Tunnels (أنفاق الغربان)

Used in: Ch2 (Lorenzo goes in through the portal), Ch5 (Sal goes in down the ventilation raise), Ch6 (start).
The "old passages" in the canon are read here as abandoned quarry mine galleries, worked 1952–1974 [proposed]. The Ravens turned them into a smuggling store.
Scale: 1 cell = 10 m, plan view of the upper level at 950 m, north up. The grid is 32 × 12 cells = 320 × 120 m.

Lower level at 910 m: from the foot of winze 8, a gallery 220 m long runs east to breakthrough 9. That is landmark 6 in `mountain-caves.md`.

**Legend**
- `#` rock. `=` gallery, 3 m wide and 2.5 m tall, with timber sets every 2 m and string lights.
- `G` generator hall. `K` bunk room. `D` depot. `g` steel gate on a winch. `~` underground stream, 2 m wide.
- `Q` quarry floor. `h` the Ravens' hut. `o` spring.

**Numbered landmarks**
1. **Ravens' hut**: concrete block, 4 × 6 m. A steel ring is set in the floor and a camp bed has been cut free of its ropes. If the owner accepts the proposal, this is where Sal's father was held.
   - In Act II the science camp fills the quarry yard: 3 tents, 2 generators, a 12 m floodlight mast.
2. **Portal**: a 4 m concrete arch with a new padlocked steel door.
3. **Depot**: 50 × 30 m. Holds:
   - pallets of cigarettes,
   - 40 fuel jerrycans, all empty (the Ravens were waiting for a delivery),
   - a cold-store box with an open lid, stencilled in a lab font. The material was kept here.
   - Sal finds the nail driver here, on the timber-shoring bench.
4. **Generator hall**:
   - a 20 kVA diesel generator,
   - a breaker panel with 3 breakers: depot, gallery and gate winch,
   - a fuel tank on a 3 m platform reached by a steel ladder.
5. **Bunk room**: 6 bunks and a card table.
6. **Ventilation raise**: a 60 m ladder in a 2 m shaft up to the surface, 300 m above the quarry. Omar escaped this way.
7. **Timber bridge**: crosses the underground stream with a 6 m drop. It collapses in Ch5.
8. **Winze**: a 40 m ladder shaft down to the lower level.
9. **Breakthrough** (lower level).

**Player goal**
- **Ch2 (Lorenzo):** follow the string-light cable to the lower level and film whatever took the Ravens. The generator has no fuel, so Lorenzo has only his camera light. Thomas's team refuels it on Day 6. In Ch5 it runs, but all 3 breakers have tripped.
- **Ch5 (Sal):**
  - Climb down the raise [6]. Take the nail driver at [3].
  - Restore 3 breakers at [4] so the gate [g] opens.
  - Cross the bridge [7], which collapses, and reach his father near the winze [8].

**Three landmarks seen from a distance**
- **Quarry terraces**: four white cuts, 15 m each, visible from the village square and the stone bridge.
- **Rusted conveyor**: 80 m long, slanting down from the portal. It is a black diagonal against the white cuts.
- **Floodlight mast**: 12 m tall over the science camp. From Day 5 it is the brightest light in the valley at night.

**Environmental story, told without words: the card table [5]**
- Five chairs. Four of them still hold their men, now wooden, with root cords grown up through the chair legs into their backs.
- The cards are still fanned in their hands.
- The fifth chair, at the head of the table, is knocked over and empty.
- A torch, a lighter and one shoe lead away from it to the ladder of the raise [6].
- There are scuff marks on every ladder rung going up, and none coming down.

**Key moments**
- **Ch2:** Lorenzo finds the voice recorder in the depot: «أغلقوا البوابة! الحيطان تتحرك!» ("Shut the gate! The walls are moving!").
- **Ch5:**
  - The 3rd breaker lights the gallery, and the roots along the string-light runs pull back 2 m. Sal says nothing; it is a hint the player may or may not notice.
  - The bridge collapses.
  - Sal finds his father pinned by roots. The prod shock makes the roots let go. This is the canon discovery, and it is scripted to teach the prod.

**Verbs here**
- Crouch under fallen timber sets (1.15 m gap).
- Use breakers and the gate winch.
- Melee and the nail driver against the **Bound**: Ravens anchored to the walls by root cords with a 6 m reach. They lunge.
- Combat against a **Knot**: a fixed root cluster that throws splinters.
- Prod after the discovery.
- Climb ladders.
