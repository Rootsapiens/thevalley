# Region: The Village (القرية)

The owner asked for this region to be built first. It is used in four chapters: Prologue, 1, 3 and 4 (see `../chapter-plan.md`).
Scale: 1 cell = 5 m. Grid is 40 × 26 cells = 200 × 130 m. North is up. The ground drops 50 m from west (920 m) to east (870 m) toward the stream.

**Legend**
- `^` pines. `t` olive terraces.
- `#` dry-stone wall, 1.0 m tall. The player can jump it, because jump height is 1.16 m.
- `-` and `|` wooden rail fence, 1.2 m tall. The player cannot jump it.
- `,` grass or mud. `.` yard or square cobbles.
- `:` footpath or gate. `=` valley road, 5 m wide. `~` the stream.
- `H` house. `B` barn or shed. `C` church nave. `S` police post. `K` café-shop. `+` graveyard. `W` stone trough. `o` stepping stone.

**Numbered landmarks** (the digit stands in one cell of the building)
1. **Farmer's big barn**: 20 × 20 m, 22 stalls, doors 4 m wide.
2. **Farmhouse**: the farmer and his wife. Her black-and-white TV can be seen through the window [ref].
3. **Bell tower**: 5 × 5 m base, 22 m tall. An internal ladder climbs 4 flights of about 5 m each. The top is the only place with phone signal.
4. **Fountain**: in the middle of the 50 × 25 m square. Next to it are an iron bench and the bucket-and-rope [ref].
5. **Café-shop**: rooms upstairs. Omar and Elia have the room on the square side, Lorenzo the room on the back side.
6. **Police post**: the old school. A police car, then Moreno's table and maps.
7. **Maryam's house**: last house before the stream. Retaining wall, 6 stone steps, a lean-to shed with one cow, a back gate at (33,5).
8. **Stepping stones**: 7 stones, 0.6 m apart, across 5 m of water. This is Joseph's shortcut to the herd trail.
9. **Transformer pole**: the end of the 10 kV line, carrying the square's only street lamp. In Chapter 4 the agency checkpoint stands here.

**Three landmarks seen from a distance** (for orienting the player)
- **(a) Bell tower [3]**: the tallest thing in the valley, visible from the herd trail and from the quarry.
- **(b) Cave mouth**: a black arch 40 m wide on the mountain face, 2.8 km to the north-east and 300 m higher than the village. Seen down the path to Maryam's gate, it fills the view.
- **(c) Ridge mast**: 30 m tall on the west ridge, red light blinking every 1.5 s. At night it marks west.

**Player goal per chapter**
- **Prologue (Joseph):** find the farmer's cow and follow the herd past the stream.
- **Ch1 (Lorenzo):** show the father's photo to 4 people, film 3 testimonies, and find Maryam.
- **Ch3 (Lorenzo):** climb the tower [3] and upload the footage before the agencies arrive.
- **Ch4 (Sal):** get past checkpoint [9], take the stun prod from barn [1], and reach the stepping stones [8].

**Environmental story, told without words: barn [1]**
- 22 stalls. Every chain is still clipped to its ring, and every halter lies in the straw still buckled. The animals slipped out of them; nothing broke.
- The straw is still warm [ref] and the trough [W] is dry [ref].
- One line of hoofprints leaves the door in single file, crosses the pasture without touching a fence, and bends north-east toward the cave mouth.
- At the farmhouse, the dog's chain is still clipped to a buckled collar, and the dog is gone.
- On barn 1's door, someone has chalked «الغربان» ("the Ravens") and drawn an arrow pointing south, the wrong way.

**Verbs here**
- Walk, crouch, jump, use (doors, gates, NPCs).
- Talk: press E on an NPC to show subtitles.
- Film (Lorenzo): hold RMB.
- Hold-use for 20 s (upload).
- Stealth past patrols (Sal). Being seen by a patrol turns him back 30 m; it does not kill him.
- Hatchet on the bramble at the back gate.
- Prod a fuse box on pole [9] to kill the checkpoint floodlights for 30 s.
- No combat in the village.
