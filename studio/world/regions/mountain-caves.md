Used in: Prologue (end), Ch2 (end), Ch6, Ch7, Ch8.

**Legend**
- `#` rock. `.` cave floor. `:` root crawlway, 1.0–1.2 m high (crouch).
- `H` Hall floor. `~` cave stream, 0.3 m deep, flowing south. `W` the Well. `X` breakthrough. `=` Ravens' gallery.
- `,` slope outside the mountain.

**Numbered landmarks**
1. **Cave mouth**: 40 m wide and 25 m tall, at 1,180 m. Snow sits on the rocks outside and meltwater pools on the floor [ref].
2. **Root crawlway**: 60 m long. The roots open and close it.
3. **The Hall**: 85 × 50 m with an 18 m ceiling. About 400 preserved animals stand in it: cows, sheep, goats, horses, deer and a dog [ref].
   - Joseph stands 6 m from the stream, one hand out toward the farmer's cow.
4. **The Well**: a shaft 30 m wide and 70 m tall. Daylight falls from a sinkhole. Roots hang from it like curtains [ref].
   - The Deep is 120 m below the Well, reached by a root-choked chimney.
5. **The Deep** (below 4, not drawn): the place where the roots meet. It is shown only as sound, heat and movement. Nobody sees all of it.
6. **Breakthrough**: where the Ravens' lower gallery cut into the cave wall. There are timber props on one side and bare rock on the other.
7. **Sink**: the cave stream drops into a slot here. It flows 900 m south underground, under the Ravens' timber bridge, and comes out at the spring beside the quarry. This is the "o" on the `ravens-tunnels.md` map.

**Player goal**
- **Prologue:** reach the cow.
- **Ch2:** film Joseph, then get out through the mouth.
- **Ch6:** open a route from 6 to 3 with the cable reel.
- **Ch7:** hold the Hall until Kira's samples are packed.
- **Ch8:** reach Sal's father at the Well while the strike brings the ceilings down.

**Three landmarks seen from a distance**
- **The mouth [1]** is visible from the whole valley. From inside, it is a bright arch that shows the way out.
- **The Well's light column [4]** shows from anywhere in the Hall, a vertical beam of daylight.
- **The cave stream** sounds louder as the player gets closer to the Hall, and every channel leads down to the sink [7].

**Environmental story, told without words**
- In the Hall the animals all face the same way: toward the Well.
- Their hoofprints in the mud stop under their feet; none lead out again.
- One cow at the edge still has a bell on its neck. When the player passes, the bell does not ring: the strap has turned to wood with the cow.

**Key moments**
- **Prologue:** the cow stops beside Joseph. Roots rise around his ankles while the player still has control. The view slowly freezes, still in first person.
- **Ch2:** Lorenzo's camera light passes over the animals one by one, and the last one is Joseph. While he is filming, the crawlway [2] closes behind him, so he leaves through the mouth [1].
- **Ch7:** Sal sees Joseph. The player knows him from the Prologue; Sal does not.
- **Ch8:** the strike begins:
  - The ceiling drops at a scripted point every 40–90 s.
  - The crawlway [2] is crushed.
  - Generator power fails at minute 6.

**Verbs here**
- Crouch through the crawlway.
- Evade root lashes. Lashes give a 0.6 s warning: a creak and falling dust.
- Film (Ch2).
- Prod: a 3 m cone that clears roots for 8 s.
- Lay cable from the reel: up to 100 m, and it keeps a 2 m corridor clear while powered.
- Hatchet on tendrils up to 10 cm thick.
- Nail driver against the Bound.
- Power 4 floodlight towers in the Hall. Floodlights let the player see; the cable under them is what keeps the roots back.
