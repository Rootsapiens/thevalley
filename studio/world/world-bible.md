# Omega Valley (وادي أوميغا) — World Bible

The source of truth is `studio/world/story-canon-ar.md`. It overrides `studio/game-brief.md`.
Tags: **[canon]** comes from the owner's canon text. **[ref]** comes from the owner's earlier games under `reference/`. **[proposed]** is a studio proposal that the owner can change. Numbers are [proposed] unless tagged otherwise.
No place in the canon has a proper name except Omega Valley, so this document does not invent names. It uses "the village (القرية)", "the mountain (الجبل)" and "the stream (الجدول)".

## 1. The valley
The valley runs north to south. It is 7 km long, and its floor is 1.5 km wide. The stream (الجدول) flows south from snowmelt at the valley head (1,300 m) and leaves through the gorge (780 m). The season is late November: rain and mud in the village, and snow above 1,400 m [ref: snowy slopes in the cave-mouth and fence art]. Sunset is at 16:50, at azimuth 240° (WSW). At the Prologue's 16:40 the sun is at 240° and 4° up, behind the west ridge and the player, so it lights the mountain face across the valley and the cave mouth reads as a black arch. The mountain and cave mouth are at bearing 45° (NE) from the village [decision: `decisions-noor.md`].

Place logic: water from the cave system crosses the Hall (region 6) and comes out at a spring beside the quarry (930 m), then joins the stream. The road follows the stream because the gorge is the only gap in the ridge. The village sits on the sunny west slope, 40 m above the floodplain. The mountain is across the water, so every trip there means crossing the stream.

## 2. The village
- **People and buildings:** 16 households and 58 residents. The core holds 12 buildings (map: `regions/village.md`). They are dry-stone houses with slate roofs and timber balconies, plus the church with icons of St George, the Madonna and the Annunciation [ref].
- **Square** (50 × 25 m): an old fountain with cold water [ref], an iron bench, and the only street lamp, which hangs on the transformer pole.
- **Who lives where:**
  - The farmer and his wife: farmhouse and big barn at the north pasture [ref]. She watches a black-and-white TV [ref].
  - The priest: the house beside the church [ref].
  - Maryam (مريم) and Joseph (جوزيف): the last house before the stream, with a yard, a stone retaining wall, and a lean-to shed for one cow [ref].
  - The café-shop on the square has two rooms upstairs. Omar (عمر) and Elia (إيليا) rent one. Lorenzo (لورينزو) takes the other.
  - The police use the old school (closed since 2009) as a forward post. Moreno's station is in the lower town, 18 km south [proposed].
- **Livestock before Night 0:** about 400 animals (cows, sheep, goats, 6 horses, dogs) across 9 barns. On Day 9 none are left [canon: "the animals have disappeared"].
- **Power:** one 10 kV line on poles along the valley road ends at the transformer pole on the square, which supplies 230 V to the houses. The east bank has no power.
- **Signal:** the slope hides the ridge mast from the village. Only the top of the bell tower gets one bar of signal.

## 3. The mountain: caves and tunnels
- **Natural caves** [canon: "a network of caves and old passages"]. The cave mouth (40 m arch) leads down an entrance slope to a root crawlway 1.0 m high, and from there to **the Hall** (85 × 50 m, 18 m ceiling, with a stream across the floor). The Hall holds about 400 preserved animals [ref: wooden animals in the great hall], and Joseph. **The Well** is a shaft 30 m wide and 70 m tall with daylight from a sinkhole above [ref: root chamber]. **The Deep** lies 120 m below the Well. That is where the roots converge, and it is never shown clearly.
- **The Ravens' tunnels** [proposed: the "old passages" are abandoned quarry mine galleries from 1952–1974]. The Ravens added timber props, string lights, a 20 kVA diesel generator, a depot, bunks and a steel gate. A lower level 40 m down breaks into the natural caves. Their digging opened that breakthrough [proposed]. A ventilation raise with a 60 m ladder leads to the surface 300 m above the quarry. Omar escapes this way [proposed].

## 4. The roots (rules)
**Canon rules**
1. The roots grow inside living creatures, reshape them and keep every detail. The result looks like wood: no blood and no torn bodies.
2. Animals walk to the mountain by themselves. They leave without breaking fences [canon]. They all turn at once [ref].
3. Humans are not called. They are taken only when they come close or enter the passages (Joseph; the Ravens).
4. Bodies the roots have taken become extensions of the network (the Ravens).
5. The passages change shape and can cut off the way out.
6. An **electric shock forces the roots back for a short time.** It does not destroy the network, and nobody knows how far the network reaches.
7. The roots are linked to the biological material that Sal's father brought. Knowing where the material came from gives no control over it.
8. The strike does not end the phenomenon. It may have moved somewhere else (Kira's forest).

**Game rules [proposed]**
- A root lash reaches 4 m and strikes at 6 m/s. A creak and falling dust warn the player 0.6 s before it strikes.
- Passive growth is 0.5 m per hour. The roots stay inside the mountain until Day 9. After that, fine root hairs show in the stream bank at the stepping stones.
- **Electricity:**
  - A stun-prod shock clears a 3 m cone for 8 s.
  - A powered circuit (string lights or a cable reel) keeps a 2 m corridor clear for as long as the generator runs.
  - When the current stops, the roots come back at 1 m/s.
  - Large root knots need generator power, because a battery is not enough.
- **Hatchet:** cuts tendrils up to 10 cm thick. A cut end grows back in 60 s.
- **Nails:** pin a tendril for 2 s.
- **Light and fire have no effect.** Electricity stays the one counter (see `conflicts.md` C7).

## 5. Factions
- **The village** keeps its distance from outsiders and blames the Ravens [ref].
- **The Ravens (الغربان)** are a smuggling gang of about 12 men led by Omar. They store goods in the mountain and kidnapped Sal's father [canon]. All of them except Omar are taken [canon].
- **The local police** are Moreno (مورينو) and his men, with Elia undercover [canon]. They lose the case to the agencies [canon].
- **Outside agencies** (unnamed) arrive after Lorenzo's footage spreads. They detain Lorenzo, take his footage, take the case and put a cordon around the valley [canon].
- **The science team** is Kira (كيرا), Thomas (توماس) and 4 others [proposed count]. Their camp is in the quarry yard [proposed].
- **The military** carries out the government's strike on the mountain while the scientists are still inside [canon].
- **Sal's family** is Sal (سال), his mother, and his father, the scientist from Iraq. They are not a faction. They pay Lorenzo [canon].

## 6. Timeline (the order is canon; the day numbers are [proposed])
- **Before:**
  - The Ravens have used the galleries for years.
  - Day −21: Sal's father arrives from Iraq with the material and is kidnapped by the Ravens.
  - Day −14: Sal and his mother hire Lorenzo.
  - Day −10 to −1: animals vanish at night ("three farms lost animals last night" [ref]). The village blames the Ravens.
- **Night 0:** Joseph follows a cow into the cave and is preserved. On the same night the tunnels become a trap. The Ravens are taken. Omar gets out with no memory.
- **Day 1:** Maryam starts searching. Lorenzo arrives. The police find Omar near the mountain.
- **Day 2:** Elia moves in with Omar, posing as his girlfriend.
- **Day 3:** Lorenzo reaches the Hall and films Joseph. That night the footage spreads.
- **Day 4:** The agencies arrive. Lorenzo is detained. The police are pushed out.
- **Days 5–7:** Kira, Thomas and the team study the roots.
- **Day 8:** Sal's father enters the mountain with the team, and Sal follows him in.
- **Day 9:** The last animals in the valley are gone. Fear of spread grows. The strike is ordered, and Moreno's warnings are overruled.
- **Day 10:** The strike. The zone is sealed and an official story is put out. What happened to the people inside is unknown.
- **After:** Joseph does not come home. Lorenzo stays detained. Omar's memory stays blank. Kira wakes in an unknown forest.

## 7. Open questions for the owner
1. How does Sal's father go from kidnapped to entering the mountain with the team? Proposal: the Ravens held him in their quarry hut, not inside the mountain. When the gang vanished he was left there, locked in. The agencies find him on Day 4 and need him because only he knows the material.
2. When does Sal arrive in the valley? Proposal: Day 8, after the footage, against the agencies' orders.
3. The Joseph game says "the roots shrink from light". The canon says electricity is the first reaction anyone can use against them. The canon wins unless the owner decides otherwise.
