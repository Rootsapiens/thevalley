# Omega Valley — Characters

The canon names are fixed. Wants follow from the canon. Secrets are [proposed] unless tagged [canon].
Acts: **I "Missing"** (Night 0 – Day 3), **II "Taken Over"** (Days 4–8), **III "The Strike"** (Days 9–10), **Epilogue "Elsewhere"**.
Each line is tagged [ref] (taken from the owner's earlier game) or [new] (written here for owner approval).

---

### Joseph (جوزيف) — the boy who looks after the animals. Playable in the Prologue.
- **Want:** to bring the farmer's cow back home.
- **Secret:** he has been crossing the stream for days, although his mother told him not to [ref: "The cow went past the stream yesterday. I saw her."].
- **Acts:**
  - I: follows the cow into the cave on Night 0 and is preserved in the Hall [canon].
  - II: still in the Hall. Lorenzo's footage of him spreads.
  - III: still in the Hall when the strike begins.
  - After: he does not come home [canon].
- **Line [ref]:** «إلى أين تذهب كلها؟» — "Where are they all going?"

### Maryam (مريم) — Joseph's mother.
- **Want:** to find her son.
- **Secret:** on Night 0 she heard hooves pass her window, bolted the door, and did not check Joseph's bed until morning.
- **Acts:**
  - I: puts up hand-drawn posters of Joseph on the lamp post and talks to Lorenzo.
  - II: refuses to leave when the village is evacuated, and shows Sal the stepping stones.
  - III: at the cordon, asking for news.
- **Line [ref; Arabic is our translation]:** «لا تتجاوز الجدول.» — "Do not go past the stream."

### Sal (سال) — son of the missing scientist. Main playable character, Chapters 4–8.
- **Want:** to bring his father home alive.
- **Secret:** his father called him the night before the kidnapping, said "if something happens, don't come looking", and Sal has never told his mother.
- **Acts:**
  - I: abroad, waiting for news from Lorenzo [canon].
  - II: arrives on Day 8, follows his father into the mountain, and finds that an electric shock makes the roots pull back [canon].
  - III: inside the mountain during the strike. He faces losing his father after reaching him [canon].
- **Line [new]:** «إنها تتراجع… الكهرباء تُرجعها!» — "It's pulling back… the current drives it back!"

### Sal's father (والد سال) — scientist from Iraq. The canon gives no name, so none is invented here.
- **Want:** to understand what the material is doing, then undo it.
- **Secret:** he knew from lab work what the material does to living tissue, and he carried it anyway.
- **Acts:**
  - Before Night 0: kidnapped by the Ravens [canon].
  - II: found and brought into the mountain with the team on Day 8. Attacked among the preserved animals [canon].
  - III: trapped with Sal and the team during the strike [canon]. What happens to him is unknown.
- **Line [new]:** «ما جلبتُه لم يكن يُفترض أن يلمس شيئاً حيّاً.» — "What I brought was never meant to touch anything alive."

### Sal's mother (والدة سال) — no name in the canon.
- **Want:** proof, for anyone, that her husband exists and is missing.
- **Secret:** the money for Lorenzo is the family's whole savings.
- **Acts:**
  - I: pays Lorenzo [canon] and calls him every evening. Her calls are phone subtitles.
  - II–III: a voice on Sal's phone, until the signal dies.
- **Line [new]:** «صوّر كل شيء يا لورينزو. لا أحد سيصدّقنا من دون صورة.» — "Film everything, Lorenzo. No one will believe us without pictures."

### Lorenzo (لورينزو) — YouTuber hired by Sal's family. Playable in Chapters 1–3.
- **Want:** to find the missing man, and a story big enough to save his channel.
- **Secret:** his channel has lost viewers for 14 months in a row. He took the job expecting to find nothing.
- **Acts:**
  - I: arrives on Day 1, interviews the village, finds Joseph and the animals on Day 3, and his footage spreads [canon].
  - II: detained, footage taken [canon].
  - III: still detained [canon].
- **Line [new]:** «إن لم أصوّره، فكأنه لم يحدث.» — "If I don't film it, it never happened."

### Omar (عمر) — leader of the Ravens. Lost his memory.
- **Want:** to know who he is.
- **Secret [canon]:** what he did and what happened to the gang is in the blank part of his memory. That includes where Sal's father was held.
- **Acts:**
  - I: the only Raven to escape. The police find him near the mountain [canon].
  - II: lives with Elia above the café and does not recognise people who say they are close to him [canon].
  - III: still blank [canon].
- **Line [new]:** «يقولون إنني أعرف هذا الجبل. لا أعرف حتى اسمي كما ينطقونه.» — "They say I know this mountain. I don't even know my name the way they say it."

### Moreno (مورينو) — local police chief.
- **Want:** to finally close the Ravens case, and later to get the scientists out alive.
- **Secret:** his informer inside the Ravens went silent on Night 0, and he has not reported it.
- **Acts:**
  - I: sees Omar and doubts the memory loss [canon]. Questions the farmers [ref: "What entrance?"].
  - II: removed from the case [canon].
  - III: tries to get warnings through and to put on record that people are still inside [canon]. These are the radio subtitles in Chapters 6–8.
- **Line [new]:** «هناك أناس في الداخل. اكتبوا ذلك في تقريركم.» — "There are people inside. Write that in your report."

### Elia (إيليا) — police officer working undercover as Omar's girlfriend.
- **Want:** to recover Omar's memories of the gang and the kidnapped scientist [canon].
- **Secret [canon]:** her identity. [proposed] She has started to like the man Omar is now.
- **Acts:**
  - I–II: at Omar's side above the café [canon].
  - III: the only person Omar talks to as the strike begins.
- **Line [new]:** «قل لي ما تتذكّره، حتى لو كان صغيراً. أنا هنا.» — "Tell me what you remember, even something small. I'm here."

### Kira (كيرا) — lead scientist [role proposed]. Playable in the Epilogue.
- **Want:** to understand the phenomenon before anyone destroys it.
- **Secret:** she took a root sample out of the camp in her own case without logging it.
- **Acts:**
  - II: finds that the roots grow inside creatures and preserve them [canon].
  - III: faces death in the Deep [canon].
  - Epilogue: wakes in an unknown forest with a troubled memory [canon].
- **Line [new]:** «إنها لا تقتل. إنها تحفظ.» — "It doesn't kill. It keeps."

### Thomas (توماس) — the science team's field engineer [role proposed]. He runs power, cable and lights.
- **Want:** to get the team out with everyone still alive.
- **Secret:** he was told the strike date on Day 8 and was ordered not to tell the team.
- **Acts:**
  - II: brings in the generator and the 100 m cable reel.
  - III: inside with Kira during the strike [canon].
- **Line [new]:** «عندي مولّد واحد وكابل بطول مئة متر. هذا كل الأمان الذي نملكه.» — "I've got one generator and a hundred meters of cable. That's all the safety we own."

### The Ravens (الغربان) — the smuggling gang, about 12 men.
- **Want:** a safe store and an untraceable route over the ridge.
- **Secret:** they kidnapped the scientist, and their digging opened the way into the natural caves [proposed].
- **Acts:**
  - Before Night 0: running the tunnels.
  - Night 0: taken by the roots and turned into parts of the network [canon].
  - II–III: their bodies are the "Bound", the anchored enemies (see `chapter-plan.md`).
- **Line [new; from a voice recorder in the depot]:** «أغلقوا البوابة! الحيطان تتحرك!» — "Shut the gate! The walls are moving!"

### The farmer [ref] and his wife [ref]
- **Want:** their herd back.
- **Secret:** he once followed a cow as far as the cave entrance and turned back [ref: "Until the entrance."].
- **Acts:**
  - I: talks to Joseph, then to Lorenzo.
  - II: evacuated.
- **Line [ref]:** «لأنها لم تهرب عنوةً. بل خرجت تمشي.» — "Because they didn't break out. They walked out."

### The priest [ref]
- **Want:** to keep the village calm and away from the mountain.
- **Secret:** he knows where the Ravens park their van at night and has never told the police.
- **Acts:**
  - I: warns Joseph, and lets Lorenzo climb the bell tower.
  - II: holds a vigil for Joseph.
- **Line [ref]:** «عُد إلى بيتك. وابتعد عن الجبل.» — "Go home. And stay away from the mountain."
